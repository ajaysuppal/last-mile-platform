# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-24

## Context
Decisions made in chat or in someone's head are lost. Reviewers (and future teammates) need to know *why* the system
looks the way it does.

## Decision
Keep short Markdown ADRs in `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md` with Context, Options, Decision and Consequences. A PR that makes a
significant decision adds or updates an ADR.

## Consequences
+ Decisions are reviewable in PRs and searchable. − Small overhead per decision.
